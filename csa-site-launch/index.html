What is corporate strategy?

The in-house team that advises the executives.

Consultants advise a company from the outside for a few months. Corporate strategists sit inside one company, report to senior leadership, and stay to see their plans carried out. The work shows up across eight roles in three groups.

Set direction

Corporate strategy & planning, corp dev & M&A, market & competitive intelligence.

Drive revenue

Commercial strategy & sales analytics, pricing & revenue management, growth & go-to-market.

Plan and operate

Strategic finance & FP&A, BizOps and strategy & operations.

What the club does

Three flagship programs a year

FallProposed

Strategy Recruiting Series

Info sessions with corporate strategy and corp dev teams during junior-internship recruiting season. Hear what each team does and who they hire.

WinterProposed

Sponsored Case Competition

A real company brings a live strategy question. Finalist teams present their recommendation to that company's strategy leaders.

SpringProposed

Utah Strategy Trek

Office visits to strategy teams along the Wasatch Front. See how planning actually happens and meet the analysts doing it.

Along with club meetings on the first and third Thursday of each month, 7:00 pm in TNRB 2325

Practitioner Speakers

Strategy and corp dev professionals on what the job actually involves.

Skills Workshops

Market sizing, Excel modeling, and executive-ready slides.

Recruiting Prep

Resume reviews, strategy case interviews, and behavioral mocks.
