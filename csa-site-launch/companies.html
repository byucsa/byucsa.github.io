Ways to engage

Ordered from lightest lift to deepest partnership.

15 minutes · one email

Post a role to Internship Scout

Send us a strategy, corp dev, or commercial internship and we'll put it in front of every member.

1 evening

Host an info session or speak

Explain what your strategy team does and who you hire, at a Thursday meeting or a dedicated session.

1 evening

Teach a skills workshop

Walk students through how your team builds a strategic plan, a pricing model, or a board deck.

Half day

Host a Strategy Trek visit

Bring a group of members to your office to meet the team and see how decisions get made.

Ongoing

Mentor or run mock interviews

Alumni and practitioners coach members through recruiting season.

2–4 weeks · flagship

Sponsor the case competition

Bring a live strategy question. Student teams work it, and finalists present to your leaders. You see how candidates think before you interview them.
