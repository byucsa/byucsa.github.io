How a role makes the list

An officer reads every posting before it goes up. If the day-to-day work wouldn't help you move onto a strategy team, it's left off.

We include

  • Corporate strategy and strategic planning
  • Corp dev, M&A, and partnerships
  • Pricing, revenue, and commercial strategy
  • Strategic finance and FP&A that advises leaders
  • BizOps and strategy & operations

We leave off

  • Generic sales and quota-carrying roles
  • Reporting-only finance and accounting
  • Postings without a clear analyst scope
  • Expired or unverifiable listings
