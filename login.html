Not a member yet?

Become a member in three steps

  1. 1Join CSA on clubs.byu.edu. Sign in with your NetID, find the Corporate Strategy Association, and click Join. Registering allows us to record your CSA attendance, which is used in Strategy program applications. Join on clubs.byu.edu →
  2. 2Pay dues when clubs.byu.edu prompts you: $15 per semester or $20 per year. Dues keep the club running and pay for food at events.
  3. 3Create your member account here with the same BYU email. It unlocks once your dues show on the club roster.

What members get

  • Internship Scout, updated daily
  • Recruiting guides and templates
  • Speaker slides
  • CSA attendance recorded for Strategy applications
  • Food at club events
