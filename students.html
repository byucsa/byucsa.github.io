CSA helps BYU students choose a strategy role, build the skills those interviews test, meet the teams that hire, and apply to the right internships on time.

Roles CSA prepares you for

What the job is, the titles to search for, what interviews test, and how CSA gets you ready for each.

Set direction

Decide where the company competes, what it buys, and how it grows.

Corporate Strategy & Planning

Build the annual strategic plan, size new markets, compare growth options, and turn the analysis into executive and board materials.

Titles to search
Strategy Analyst · Strategic Planning Analyst · Corporate Strategy Rotational Program
Interviews test
Structured problem solving, market sizing, and a clear recommendation.
How CSA helps
Strategy cases, executive-slide workshops, info sessions with in-house strategy teams.

Corp Dev & M&A

Screen acquisition targets, build valuation models, support due diligence, and evaluate partnerships.

Titles to search
Corporate Development Analyst · M&A Analyst · Corp Dev Intern
Interviews test
Valuation, Excel modeling, and judgment on whether a deal fits the strategy.
How CSA helps
Modeling workshops, corp dev cases in the Casing Preparation Agent.

Market & Competitive Intelligence

Track competitors, size markets, and turn industry and customer research into implications for strategy.

Titles to search
Market Intelligence Analyst · Competitive Intelligence Analyst · Insights Analyst
Interviews test
Research synthesis, market sizing, and pulling the “so what” out of messy information.
How CSA helps
Market sizing practice, research and synthesis workshops.

Drive revenue

Decide how, where, and at what price the company sells.

Commercial Strategy & Sales Analytics

Analyze sales by customer, channel, and region, build forecasts and dashboards, and recommend where the sales team should focus.

Titles to search
Sales Analyst · Commercial Analyst · Sales Strategy & Operations · Revenue Operations
Interviews test
Quick math, comfort with large datasets in Excel or SQL, and explaining data to sales leaders.
How CSA helps
Data-analysis workshops, commercial cases, Scout listings for sales and commercial roles.

Pricing & Revenue Management

Set and test prices, promotions, and discounts, and measure their effect on margin and volume.

Titles to search
Pricing Analyst · Revenue Management Analyst · Revenue Growth Management
Interviews test
Pricing and margin math, elasticity logic, and defending a price change.
How CSA helps
Pricing cases, Excel modeling workshops.

Growth & Go-to-Market

Plan launches and market entries, choose customer segments and channels, and measure growth experiments.

Titles to search
Growth Analyst · Go-to-Market Strategy Analyst · Business Development Analyst
Interviews test
Market-entry logic, customer segmentation, and picking the right metrics.
How CSA helps
Market-entry cases, speakers from growth and product teams.

Plan and operate

Forecast the numbers and help leadership execute the plan.

Strategic Finance & FP&A

Forecast the business, run planning and budgeting cycles, and review KPIs with executives.

Titles to search
FP&A Analyst · Finance Leadership Program · Strategic Finance Analyst
Interviews test
Financial modeling, business judgment, and turning numbers into a clear story.
How CSA helps
Modeling workshops, speakers from finance teams.

BizOps / Strategy & Operations

Run cross-functional projects for the COO, fix operating bottlenecks, and manage the planning cadence.

Titles to search
BizOps Analyst · Strategy & Operations Associate
Interviews test
Problem solving, prioritization, and getting things done across teams.
How CSA helps
Case practice, speakers from operations and BizOps teams.

Questions we get

What roles can I land straight out of college?

Plenty. Commercial strategy, pricing, strategic finance, BizOps, and market intelligence roles all hire undergrads, and they build the experience strategy teams look for. CSA helps you pick the right target and get ready for it.

Do I need to be a Strategy major?

No. CSA is open to every major. Finance, economics, IS, and marketing students all land in these roles.

What about consulting?

Many CSA members also recruit for consulting, and the skills overlap: structured problem solving, cases, and clear recommendations. CSA focuses on in-house roles; MCA runs consulting recruiting, and many students belong to both.

How is CSA different from MCA?

MCA prepares students for consulting recruiting. CSA focuses on in-house roles: corporate strategy, corp dev, commercial strategy, and strategic finance. Some students belong to both.

Is CSA just for underclassmen?

No. CSA is built around recruiting: Internship Scout, case practice, and company events are aimed at students actively going after internships and full-time offers. Underclassmen are welcome, and the earlier you pick a target role, the better.

Why register and pay dues?

Dues keep the club running and pay for food at events. Registered, paid members get Internship Scout, the Casing Preparation Agent, and the resource library. Registering allows us to record your CSA attendance, which is used in Strategy program applications. Club meetings and the Slack stay open to everyone.
